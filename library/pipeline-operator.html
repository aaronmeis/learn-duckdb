Notes

  • --skip-existing on nlm download all avoids re-pulling finished artifacts.
  • Slide decks need both PDF (--slide-format pdf) and PPTX (nlm download slide-deck … --format pptx). download all keeps only one slide format, so the PPTX step is required.
  • vault-file copies light .md / .csv / .json into notebooklm/exports/ and slide JPGs into assets/presentation/ + Presentation slides; audio/video/PDF/PPTX stay under the heavy output path (inventory points at them).
