DuckDB — light research

Date: 2026-10-10 Purpose: Source the one-day mastery pack. Search only. No deep report.

Key takeaways

  • The anchor paper is Raasveldt and Mühleisen, SIGMOD 2019, "DuckDB: an Embeddable Analytical Database" (505 citations in this search).
  • MonetDBLite (SIGMOD 2018, same authors) is the embedded predecessor. DuckDB is the system the day should teach.
  • Vectorized execution is older than DuckDB. Boncz's 2024 SIGMOD companion keynote treats it as a 20-year technique. DuckDB uses it. The day should not treat the idea as DuckDB-only.
  • Later papers (filter representation, chunk compaction, Python UDFs) are edge reading, not the core day.
