Not a DuckDB certification

Smaug (2023) and BiTDB (2024) study SQLite or embedded databases in a TEE. They are on the ledger so the closed-lab question has a negative result: no paper in this pack evaluates DuckDB in a classified lab.

Rejected as an authority

  • Slide claims of "10×" or "a billion rows in 157 ms" are briefing lines, not an SLA.
  • DuckLake on a slide is not the concurrency contract. The contract is the concurrency doc.
  • A secondary blog's claim about MotherDuck regions is not a MotherDuck commitment until it is on MotherDuck's regions page.
