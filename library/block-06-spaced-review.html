Block 6: Spaced review

Time: 20 minutes
Goal: Retrieval without re-reading
Pack: DuckDB (duckdb)

Focus from mind map

  • Day outcome: By the end of the day, explain DuckDB from zero and sketch one local analytics query you could defend. The sketch names the storage layout, the execution model, and when DuckDB beats Postgres, SQLite, Polars, or Spark. Success is a short written explanation plus that sketch, with one named failure mode.
  • Depth: Working fluency across the engine, not a survey of every SQL function and not a single-subsystem deep dive. Enough to sketch a scan-join-aggregate and say why the engine runs it that way. A later day can go deep on one slice (vectorized execution, or the storage format).
  • Prior knowledge: Assume zero. Define a table, a query, a join, OLTP versus OLAP, and row storage versus column storage before any DuckDB-specific term. Do not assume the CLI, Parquet, or EXPLAIN.
  • Boundaries: Must include: what DuckDB is (in-process SQL database), row versus column storage, vectorized execution, morsel-driven parallelism, the single-file database, reading Parquet and CSV, EXPLAIN at a sketch level, extensions in outline, and when not to use DuckDB.
